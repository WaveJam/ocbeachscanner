Overview

OC Beach Scanner, operated by WaveJam Technologies LLC, provides public-safety audio, incident awareness, traffic-camera views, weather, tide, surf, radar, and related information for Ocean City, Maryland and the surrounding area. We respect your privacy and designed the iOS app to operate without requiring an account or asking for personal information.

Information the app does not request

The OC Beach Scanner iOS app does not require an account and does not request access to your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, health data, or advertising identifier. The app does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network and technical information

The app must connect to internet services to provide live audio and current information. When your device makes those requests, our infrastructure and service providers may automatically receive standard technical information such as an IP address, request time, requested resource, and user-agent or similar connection details.

OC Beach Scanner may retain limited server-log information when reasonably necessary for service reliability, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. We do not sell this information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to build profiles of app users.

Third-party information services

The app retrieves information from public or third-party services, including the National Weather Service, NOAA, Maryland CHART, sunrise-sunset.org, Broadcastify, and related public-facing data sources. Requests to those services are subject to their own privacy practices and terms. These services may receive standard network information necessary to respond to a request.

Beach conditions use fixed Ocean City coordinates built into the app. The app does not request your device's location to provide these reports.

CarPlay and audio

CarPlay and background-audio features are used only to provide and control OC Beach Scanner audio playback. The app does not use CarPlay to collect personal information.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share app-user information for advertising or cross-app tracking. Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect the security or integrity of the service, or to service providers that are necessary to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

The app does not create a user account or maintain a personal profile for you. Routine technical server logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operational, security, troubleshooting, or legal purposes. If you believe we hold information associated with you and want to ask about access or deletion, contact us using the address below.

Children

OC Beach Scanner is a general-audience information service and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or our services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
